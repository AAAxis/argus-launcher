Privacy Policy

Data Collection

This extension collects minimal data necessary for its operation:

  • Your account code for license verification
  • Proxy connection status
  • Extension settings and preferences

Data Usage

Your data is used to:

  • Verify your Pro subscription
  • Maintain the proxy connection
  • Store your preferences locally

Data Storage

Settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. Proxy credentials are held in memory only and are never written to disk.

Third-Party Services

Traffic you choose to route is sent through your FoxyWall proxy server. Please review the applicable privacy terms separately.
